Delivery policy

Last updated 24 September 2026

Atlitos is operated by ELSHEPH SYSTEMS INDIA PRIVATE LIMITED. Everything you can pay for on Atlitos today is a service or a donation, so nothing is shipped. This page explains how and when you receive what you paid for.

No physical goods are sold or shipped. There is nothing to post, no courier, and no delivery charge. If we begin selling physical goods, this page will state the charges and timelines before any order can be placed.

1. Coaching sessions

A coaching session is delivered in person, at the date, time and place agreed with the coach and shown on the booking. Confirmation is immediate: once the coach accepts and payment is captured, the session appears in your Trainings with the slot and the location. Nothing is posted to you.

If a coach has not answered a request, the session is not yet confirmed and you can cancel it for a full automatic refund. See the refund and cancellation policy.

2. Court bookings

A court booking is delivered as access to the court for the slot you booked. Confirmation is immediate on payment, and the booking, the venue and the slot appear in the app. Arrive at the venue at the booked time. Nothing is posted to you.

3. Atlitos Life donations

A donation is passed to the verified athlete you chose to support. There is no physical fulfilment. Your donation and its effect appear in My Impact in the app, usually within a few minutes of payment.

4. Digital access

Drills, training content and anything else you unlock in the app are available immediately on your account after payment. If something you paid for has not appeared within an hour, write to support@elsheph.com and we will fix it or refund it.

5. Where we operate

Atlitos operates in India. Coaches, venues and athletes are in India, prices are in Indian Rupees, and payments are processed in India through Razorpay.
