Support

How to reach us

We are a small team and we read every message. The fastest way to reach a person is email.

Email support@elsheph.com

We aim to reply within two working days.

What to include

To get you an answer in one reply rather than three, tell us:

  • The email address on your Atlitos account.
  • What you were trying to do, and what happened instead.
  • Your device and whether you are on iPhone or Android.
  • For anything involving money, the booking, session, order, or donation it relates to.

Bookings, sessions, and orders

Cancellation and refund terms depend on what you booked and how close to the start time you cancel. The app shows the applicable terms before you confirm and pay. If a refund is due and has not arrived, email us with the booking details and we will trace it.

Payments

Payments are handled by Razorpay. We never see or store your full card or bank details. If a payment was taken but your booking did not appear, contact us and we will reconcile it.

Reporting content or a person

Every clip, comment, chat message, and profile in Atlitos can be reported from inside the app, and you can block another user from their profile. Reported content goes to our moderation queue. If something is urgent or involves someone's safety, email us directly and mark it urgent.

Your account and your data

To correct your details, edit your profile in the app. To ask for your account and data to be deleted, see the account deletion page. To understand what we collect and why, see our privacy policy.
