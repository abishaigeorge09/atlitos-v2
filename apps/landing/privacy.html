Privacy policy

Last updated 30 September 2026

This explains what Atlitos collects, why we collect it, who else receives it, how long we keep it, and how you delete it. It is written to match what the app actually does.

The short version. We collect what the app needs to work: your profile, what you book and pay for, what you post, and who you talk to. We do not sell your data. We do not track you across other apps or websites. We record which retailer offers you open, to measure the shop. We carry no advertising or third party analytics software. You can delete your account from inside the app at any time.

Who we are

Atlitos is operated by ELSHEPH SYSTEMS INDIA PRIVATE LIMITED. For any privacy question, or to raise a grievance under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, write to support@elsheph.com. We respond to data requests within 30 days.

What we never collect

We do not collect your card number, CVV, UPI PIN or bank credentials. Those go directly to our payment provider and never reach our servers. We record which retailer offers you open, to measure the shop. We carry no advertising or third party analytics software, and no cross app tracking.

How long we keep it, and what deletion actually does

You can delete your account at any time: Settings, then Account, then Delete account, then confirm by typing DELETE. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

When you delete your account, we remove:

  • your profile, photo, phone number, date of birth and location
  • every video, caption, comment and like you posted
  • who you follow and who follows you
  • your chat messages
  • your saved addresses, cart and wishlists
  • your notifications and push tokens
  • your coaching certificates and any Atlitos Life application and documents
  • photos you uploaded for venues you manage
  • your training progress

What we keep, and why. We keep the record of payments, refunds, payouts and donations. Indian tax and accounting law requires us to retain transaction records, and our financial books have to balance. Those records stay attached to an anonymous entry rather than to you: your name, phone number and other details are erased from them. They identify a transaction, not a person.

If we paid you as a coach or venue partner, the payout record keeps only the last four digits of your bank account number and your bank's IFSC, so a past payout can still be matched to a bank statement. Your name, your PAN, the rest of the account number, and your UPI ID apart from the bank handle after the @ are erased.

We also keep reports you filed about other members. Otherwise anyone could erase the evidence against an account by deleting their own.

While your account is open, we keep your data for as long as you use Atlitos. Your sign in identity is disabled immediately on deletion and the email address is released, so it can be used for a new account later.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act you can:

  • See what we hold about you. Ask us and we will send it.
  • Correct it. Most of it you can edit yourself in the app.
  • Delete it. Use Delete account in the app, or ask us.
  • Withdraw consent. Turn off location or notifications in your device settings at any time. The rest of the app keeps working. To withdraw consent entirely, delete your account.
  • Nominate someone. You can nominate a person to exercise these rights for you in the event of your death or incapacity. Write to us to do so.
  • Complain. Write to us first at support@elsheph.com. You may also complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Age

You must be at least 18 to create an Atlitos account. Atlitos is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will remove it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access to your rows is enforced by the database itself, not only by the app, so another member cannot read your data by changing an identifier in a request. Private files, including your videos and any verification documents, are served through short lived signed links rather than public addresses.

No system is perfect. If a breach affects you we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Where your data is processed

Our database and files are hosted by Supabase in Mumbai, India (AWS ap-south-1). Payments are processed by Razorpay in India. Some processing, such as search, crash reports and app delivery, may take place outside India, protected by contractual safeguards with each provider.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
