Privacy Policy

Effective 7 August 2026

This policy explains what information the Atlitos mobile app and website ("Atlitos", "we", "us") collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. Atlitos is operated by ELSHEPH SYSTEMS INDIA PRIVATE LIMITED. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use Atlitos.

Information we collect

We collect only what the app needs to work. The categories below reflect the features you choose to use.

Account and profile

  • Your email address and password, used to create and secure your account.
  • Profile details you provide: name, sports you play, city and state, and a profile photo if you upload one.

Payments

  • Payments for court bookings, coaching, shop orders, and donations are processed by our payment provider, Razorpay. We do not receive or store your full card or bank details.
  • We store transaction records (amount, status, and the related booking, session, order, or donation) so we can show your history, issue refunds, and keep required financial records.
  • If you are a coach or partner, we store payout account references needed to pay you.

Location

  • With your permission, we use your device location to find and sort sports courts near you. You can deny or revoke this permission in your device settings; the rest of the app still works.

Camera, photos, and microphone

  • With your permission, we access your camera, photo library, and microphone so you can capture and upload clips ("Clutch") and profile photos. Clips are video with audio.

Content you create

  • Clips, comments, chat messages, and posts you submit. Chat message content is stored so conversations are available to you and the people you message.

Commerce

  • Shipping addresses and order history when you purchase from the in-app shop.

Notifications and device

  • A push notification token, if you enable notifications, so we can alert you about bookings, messages, and funding activity. You can turn notifications off in your device settings.
  • Your notification and personalization preferences.

How we use your information

  • To operate core features: bookings, coaching, the shop, Clutch, chat, and donations.
  • To process payments and refunds through Razorpay and to keep financial records.
  • To send notifications you have opted into.
  • To keep the community safe: to review reports, act on blocked users, and moderate content.
  • To maintain, secure, and improve the service, and to comply with applicable law.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers that help us run Atlitos, and only as needed:

  • Supabase, our database, authentication, storage, and hosting provider.
  • Razorpay, our payment processor.
  • Expo, to deliver push notifications.
  • Anthropic, when AI-assisted search is enabled, to interpret your search query. Search queries may be processed by this provider; do not include sensitive personal details in search.
  • Authorities or others when required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and property of our users and Atlitos.

Data retention

We keep your account and content while your account is active. Financial and transaction records are retained for as long as required by applicable law and tax rules, even after account deletion. When you ask us to delete your account, we remove your personal data on the timeline described on our account deletion page, except records we must keep by law.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction: you can view and edit most profile information in the app.
  • Deletion: you can request deletion of your account and associated data. See our account deletion page.
  • Permissions: you can grant or revoke location, camera, photo, microphone, and notification access at any time in your device settings.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including access controls at the database level, encryption of data in transit, and short-lived, signed access to private media. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

Atlitos is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the effective date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
