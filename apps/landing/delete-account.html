Delete your account and data

Effective 7 August 2026

You can ask us to delete your Atlitos account and the personal data associated with it. This page explains how to request deletion, what is removed, and what we are required to keep.

How to request deletion

Email support@elsheph.com from the email address on your Atlitos account, with the subject line Delete my account. Include your account email so we can verify the request.

Email a deletion request

We verify that the request comes from the account owner before deleting anything, so we may reply to confirm your identity.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile: name, sports, city and state, avatar, and personalization and notification preferences.
  • Content you created: your clips, comments, chat messages, and posts.
  • Uploaded media: profile photos and clip video files.
  • Your saved shipping addresses.
  • Your push notification tokens.

What we keep, and why

We retain a limited set of records after deletion where the law requires it:

  • Financial and transaction records (payments, refunds, payouts, donations, and the associated ledger entries) are kept for the period required by applicable tax and accounting law. Where possible these are disassociated from your profile.
  • Records we must keep to resolve disputes, prevent fraud, or comply with a legal obligation.

Timeline

We action verified deletion requests within 30 days and email you when it is complete. Records we are legally required to keep are retained only for the period the law requires, and then deleted.
